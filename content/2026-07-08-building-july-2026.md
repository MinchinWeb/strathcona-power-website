## Pricing Update

This section is general notes on electricity and natural gas pricing in
Alberta, looking back at June and forward to July.

The floating prices continue to be very low for both
[power]({filename}/pages/EL/floating-electricity.md) and
[gas]({filename}/pages/NG/floating-natural-gas.md), and so I remain a fan of
both. Fixed pricing seems to have bottomed out (for now?) in light of data
center announcements[^2026070801], and so if you want to sign up for a [fixed
gas prices]({filename}/pages/NG/fixed-natural-gas-1-yr.md) for next winter,
this might be a good time to do so.
