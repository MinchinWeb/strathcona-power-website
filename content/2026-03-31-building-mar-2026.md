## Pricing Update

Normally I would speak to March prices, but I've just set my April
(this afternoon) so those are probably more helpful.

The floating prices has been very low for both
[power]({filename}/pages/EL/floating-electricity.md) and
[gas]({filename}/pages/NG/floating-natural-gas.md) over the last month, so
[fixed power rates]({filename}/pages/EL/fixed-electricity-5-yr.md) have started
to come down (some as much as 1 &cent;/kWh over the last two months), but
[fixed gas prices]({filename}/pages/NG/fixed-natural-gas-5-yr.md) are
bascically flat.

Personally, I'm still a fan of floating prices for both power and gas.
