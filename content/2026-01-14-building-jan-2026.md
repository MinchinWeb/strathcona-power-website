## Pricing Update

Overall, December was very cold, and although Alberta set a new peak electrical
demand (at 12,785 MW), realtime power and gas prices have remained
comparatively week. Wholesale prices for power over the last month came in at
4.067 &cent;/kWh and wholesale gas came in at $3.18/GJ.

In looking at longer term hedges, prices rise as you go further out, with 2028
(or later) wholesale hedges coming in >11 &cent;/kWh. Largely, this is likely
driven by the expected data centre boom and related expected capacity squeeze.

Our pricing for January:

| Term                  |     Electricity | Natural Gas |

| Floating[^2026011401] | ~5.1 &cent;/kWh |   ~$4.07/GJ |

Overall, I continue to suggest [floating for
electricity]({filename}/pages/floating-electricity.md). For natural gas, the [1
year fixed price]({filename}/pages/fixed-natural-gas-1-yr.md) looks like the
way to go.
