## Pricing Update

This section is general notes on electricity and natural gas pricing in
Alberta, looking back at April and forward to May.

The floating prices continue to be very low for both
[power]({filename}/pages/floating-electricity.md) and
[gas]({filename}/pages/floating-natural-gas.md), and so I remain a fan of both.
Fixed pricing continues to drop, and so if you want to sign up for a [fixed gas
prices]({filename}/pages/fixed-natural-gas-1-yr.md) for next winter, this might
be a good time to do so.
