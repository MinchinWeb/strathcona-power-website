Alberta has a vey generous microgeneration regulation that allows you to sell
your extra solar power produced back to the grid!

One condition of the rate structure is the retail price you pay is *the same
rate you get paid for exported electricity*.

To gain the most benefit from this, you can set your rate as high as possible
during the summer months (when you are a net exporter) and as low as possible
during the winter months (when you are a net consumer). This is sometimes
called a **Solar Club Rate.**
