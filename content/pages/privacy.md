## Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Strathcona Power Ltd. is committed to safeguarding the personal information
entrusted to us by our customers. We manage your personal information in
accordance with Alberta’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and other
applicable laws. This policy outlines the principles and practices we follow in
protecting your personal information.

This policy applies to Strathcona Power Ltd. and to any person providing
services on our behalf. A copy of this policy is provided to any customer on
request and is available on our website.

## What is Personal Information?

Personal information means information about an identifiable individual. This
includes an individual’s name, home address and phone number, age, sex, marital
or family status, identifying numbers, financial information, energy
consumption data, billing history, payment information, and credit information.

## What Personal Information Do We Collect?

We collect only the personal information that we need for the purposes of
providing energy services to our customers, including:

**For Account Management**:

- Name, address, telephone number, and email address
- Date of birth and identification verification information
- Banking and/or credit card information for payment processing
- Credit history and financial information for credit assessment

**For Service Delivery**:

- Energy consumption data and usage patterns, including historical usage
  information and historical bills provided by you, from Distribution Facility
  Owners (DFO's) or Wire Service Providers (WSP's)
- Meter readings and premise information
- Billing and payment history
- Service connection and disconnection records
- Site identification numbers (Site IDs) and meter numbers to uniquely identify
  your site

**For Customer Service**:

- Communication preferences
- Customer service inquiries and complaints
- Survey responses and feedback
- Information submitted via a form on our website or through a Facebook or
  Instagram lead ad
- Interacts with our advertising on third-party platforms (e.g., Meta,
  Facebook, Instagram, Google)
    - your interactions with Meta, Facebook, and Instrgram are governed by
      their privacy policy at <https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy>
- Emergency contact information

**For Regulatory Compliance**:

- Information required by Service Alberta, the Alberta Utilities Commission or
  any other applicable regulator
- Information required for settlement and reporting purposes
- Information needed for emergency situations

**For Website Operations**:

- Analytics, including technical information (such as IP address, browser type,
  and device information), about how you access and interact with our website.
  Some of this information is collected using cookies or similiar tracking
  technologies.

We normally collect customer information directly from our customers. We may
collect your information from other persons with your consent or as authorized
by law, including:

- Credit reporting agencies
- Previous energy retailers
- Distribution companies
- Regulatory authorities

We inform our customers, before or at the time of collecting personal
information, of the purposes for which we are collecting the information,
except when you volunteer information for an obvious purpose (such as providing
payment information to process a transaction).

## Use of Service Providers Outside Canada

Strathcona Power Ltd. may engage service providers located outside Canada,
particularly in the United States, for:

- Data storage and backup services
- Customer billing and payment processing
- Customer service support
- Technology maintenance and support
- Advertising and marketing efforts and support
- Emergency communication services


When we use service providers outside Canada, your personal information may be
subject to the laws of those jurisdictions and may be accessible by courts, law
enforcement, and national security authorities of that jurisdiction.

## Consent

We ask for consent to collect, use, or disclose customer personal information,
except in specific circumstances where collection, use, or disclosure without
consent is authorized or required by law.

**Types of Consent:**

- Express consent: Required for sensitive personal information, such as
  financial or credit information
- Implied consent: May be used when the purpose is obvious, such as providing
  your address for service connection
- Opt-out consent: May be used for marketing communications and non-sensitive
  information sharing

Withdrawing Consent: You may withdraw consent to the use and disclosure of
personal information at any time, unless the personal information is necessary
for us to fulfill our legal obligations. We will respect your decision, but we
may not be able to provide you with certain products and services if we do not
have the necessary personal information.

We may collect, use, or disclose customer personal information without consent
only as authorized by law, including:

- Emergency situations that threaten life, health, or safety
- Collection of debts owed to our organization
- Compliance with court orders or legal investigations
- Meeting regulatory requirements

## How We Use and Disclose Personal Information Information?

We use and disclose customer personal information only for the purposes for
which the information was collected, except as authorized by law:

Primary Uses:

- Establishing and maintaining your energy service account
- Providing a (free) bill review and identify potential savings on your energy
  account
- Contacting you regarding our products, services, and offers
- Billing and payment processing
- Customer service and support
- Energy delivery and meter reading
- Credit assessment and collection activities
- Regulatory reporting and compliance
- Improve our website, marketing, and services
- Disclosure to Third Parties: We may disclose your personal information to:
    - Distribution companies for service connection/disconnection
    - Credit reporting agencies
    - Collection agencies for overdue accounts
    - Government agencies and regulators as required by law
    - Service providers performing functions on our behalf
    - Emergency services in situations threatening life, health, or safety

Any disclosure is made on a strictly confidential basis in accordance with
applicable laws, and information is used only for the purpose for which it was
shared.

If we wish to use or disclose your personal information for any new business
purpose, we will ask for your consent.

## How We Safeguard Personal Information

**Accuracy**: We make every reasonable effort to ensure that customer
information is accurate and complete. We rely on customers to notify us of
changes to their personal information. You may request correction of errors at
any time.

**Security Measures**: We protect customer personal information with security
safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information, including:

- Physical security measures at our facilities
- Technological safeguards for electronic data
- Administrative controls limiting employee access on a “need to know” basis
- Secure disposal of records containing personal information

**Data Breach Response**: We will notify the Office of the Information and
Privacy Commissioner of Alberta without unreasonable delay of any security
breach affecting personal information that creates a real risk of significant
harm to individuals.

**Retention**: We retain customer personal information only as long as
reasonable to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected or for legal or
business purposes. We destroy or render non-identifying all records containing
personal information once it is no longer needed.

## Access to Your Personal Information

You have the right to access your personal information in our custody or
control, subject to some exceptions under PIPA. We may refuse access to
information that:

- Would reveal personal information about another individual
- Would reveal confidential business information
- Is subject to legal privilege
- Is contained in mediation records
