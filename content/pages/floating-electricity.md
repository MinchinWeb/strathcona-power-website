#### Why Strathcona Power?

Run by Edmontonians William and Marcy Minchin and headquartered from old
Strathcona, Strathcona Power is an Edmonton, Alberta based retailer of power
(electricity) and natural gas.

William and Marcy have been doing this since 2012, and have been around long
enough to see the cycles of the market and pricing. Through it all, this has
provided a way to both support out family and support our neighbourhood and
Alberta families more generally.

Our goal is to provide simple, quality service at affordable prices. Thanks for
your part in helping this become a reality.
