## Pricing Update

This section is general notes on electricity and natural gas pricing in
Alberta, looking back at August and forward to September.

The [floating price for
electricity]({filename}/pages/EL/floating-electricity.md) is bouncing off of
the lowest price in a decade two months ago. So it's up again from last month,
but still incredibly affordable at ~5.4 &cent;/kWh. [Floating natural
gas]({filename}/pages/NG/floating-natural-gas.md) is low as well, and slightly
lower than last month (again), but tighter (to fixed prices) at ~$1/GJ below
fixed prices. In short, I remain a fan of both and hope you are signed up on
floating rates.

Fixed prices are relatively low, and have fallen across the board this month.
Natural gas is getting very cheap for shorter terms, and so if you want to look
in the [1 year rate]({filename}/pages/NG/fixed-natural-gas-1-yr.md) for the
winter, now would be a good time.
