## Pricing Update

Pricing has been up and down over the last month, but the biggest changes have
been a drop in the 5 year electricity hedges.

Personally, I'm still a fan of floating prices for both
[power]({filename}/pages/EL/floating-electricity.md) and
[gas]({filename}/pages/NG/floating-natural-gas.md).

Our pricing for February:
